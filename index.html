Ben Kelly Rocks

My Journey

It all started when I was 12 and wanted to learn how to record videos, edit, and design thumbnails. That’s when I began my self-taught journey.

Since then, I’ve specialized in graphic design, web design, and sound production — combining creativity with technical skill to build engaging digital experiences.

What I Offer

My Services

Web Design: From sleek portfolios to blog sites, I create responsive websites that showcase your work and personality. I focus on clean layouts, intuitive navigation, and mobile-friendly design to ensure your site looks great on any device without sacrificing your personal brand and vision.

Graphic Design: Whether you need a logo, a flyer, or a full social media package, I can create designs that are both visually appealing and effective on various platforms. I'll work closely with you to make sure your vision is reflected in the final product, and that it resonates with your target audience.

Sound Design: Whether you're a YouTuber and need background music for your videos or you're an artist that needs your songs mixed and mastered, I can create immersive audio experiences that matches your style.
